# Drug Interactions Reference — Cardiovascular Medications
## Version 2024.1

### ACE Inhibitors (ramipril, lisinopril, enalapril)

| Interacting Drug | Severity | Effect | Recommendation |
|-----------------|----------|--------|----------------|
| Potassium supplements | High | Hyperkalaemia | Monitor K+ closely; avoid if K+ > 5.0 |
| Spironolactone | High | Hyperkalaemia | Specialist supervision; monitor K+ weekly initially |
| NSAIDs (ibuprofen, naproxen) | Moderate | Reduced antihypertensive effect; AKI risk | Avoid long-term; short courses acceptable |
| Lithium | High | Lithium toxicity | Monitor levels; reduce lithium dose by 25–50% |
| Aliskiren | Contraindicated | Dual RAS blockade — hyperkalaemia, renal failure | Never combine |
| Trimethoprim | Moderate | Hyperkalaemia | Monitor K+ within 1 week of starting |

### ARBs (losartan, valsartan, candesartan)

| Interacting Drug | Severity | Effect | Recommendation |
|-----------------|----------|--------|----------------|
| Potassium supplements | High | Hyperkalaemia | Same as ACE inhibitors |
| NSAIDs | Moderate | Reduced BP lowering; AKI risk | Avoid chronic use |
| ACE inhibitors | High | Dual RAS blockade — no added benefit, more harm | Do NOT combine |
| Lithium | High | Lithium toxicity | Monitor levels closely |
| Ciclosporin | High | Hyperkalaemia, nephrotoxicity | Monitor renal function and K+ |

### Calcium Channel Blockers (amlodipine, nifedipine, diltiazem, verapamil)

| Interacting Drug | Severity | Effect | Recommendation |
|-----------------|----------|--------|----------------|
| Simvastatin | High (amlodipine) | Myopathy risk — simvastatin exposure increased | Limit simvastatin to 20 mg with amlodipine |
| Beta-blockers | High (diltiazem/verapamil) | Severe bradycardia, heart block | Avoid diltiazem/verapamil + beta-blocker |
| Grapefruit juice | Moderate | Increased CCB levels | Avoid grapefruit with felodipine/nifedipine |
| CYP3A4 inhibitors | High (diltiazem/verapamil) | Increased drug levels of co-administered drugs | Check all CYP3A4 substrates |
| Dantrolene | Contraindicated (verapamil) | Fatal cardiovascular collapse | Never combine IV verapamil + dantrolene |

### Statins (atorvastatin, rosuvastatin, simvastatin)

| Interacting Drug | Severity | Effect | Recommendation |
|-----------------|----------|--------|----------------|
| Macrolide antibiotics (erythromycin, clarithromycin) | High | Rhabdomyolysis (with simvastatin/atorvastatin) | Withhold statin during course or use azithromycin |
| Ciclosporin | Contraindicated (simvastatin) | Severe rhabdomyolysis | Use pravastatin or rosuvastatin at lowest dose |
| Fibrates (gemfibrozil) | High | Myopathy | Avoid gemfibrozil; fenofibrate is safer |
| Amlodipine | Moderate | Increased simvastatin exposure | Limit simvastatin to 20 mg |
| Warfarin | Moderate | Increased INR | Monitor INR when starting/stopping statin |
| Grapefruit juice (> 1L/day) | Moderate (simvastatin/atorvastatin) | Increased statin levels | Counsel patients; small amounts acceptable |

### SGLT2 Inhibitors (empagliflozin, dapagliflozin, canagliflozin)

| Interacting Drug | Severity | Effect | Recommendation |
|-----------------|----------|--------|----------------|
| Loop diuretics (furosemide) | Moderate | Volume depletion, hypotension | Reduce diuretic dose; monitor BP |
| Insulin / sulfonylureas | Moderate | Hypoglycaemia | Consider dose reduction of insulin/SU by 20% |
| Lithium | Moderate | Altered lithium clearance | Monitor lithium levels |
| ACE inhibitors/ARBs | Low | Additive BP lowering | Monitor; usually beneficial |

### Anticoagulants (warfarin, apixaban, rivaroxaban, edoxaban)

| Interacting Drug | Severity | Effect | Recommendation |
|-----------------|----------|--------|----------------|
| NSAIDs | High | Major bleeding | Avoid combination; use paracetamol for pain |
| Antiplatelet agents (aspirin, clopidogrel) | High | Increased bleeding | Use only if clear indication (e.g., post-ACS); add PPI |
| Amiodarone | High (warfarin) | Increased INR by 30–50% | Reduce warfarin dose by 30%; monitor INR |
| Strong CYP3A4 inhibitors (ketoconazole, ritonavir) | Contraindicated (apixaban/rivaroxaban) | Markedly increased DOAC levels | Use alternative antifungal/antiviral |
| Rifampicin | High | Markedly reduced DOAC levels | Contraindicated with DOACs; use warfarin with INR monitoring |
| SSRIs (fluoxetine, sertraline) | Moderate | Increased bleeding risk (platelet inhibition) | Consider adding PPI; monitor for bruising |

Sources: BNF Interactions 2024, Stockley's Drug Interactions 13th ed., UpToDate 2024
