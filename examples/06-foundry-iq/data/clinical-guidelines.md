# Clinical Practice Guidelines — Cardiovascular & Metabolic Diseases
## Version 2024.2

### 1. Hypertension Management

#### Classification
| Category | Systolic (mmHg) | Diastolic (mmHg) |
|----------|----------------|-----------------|
| Normal | < 120 | < 80 |
| Elevated | 120–129 | < 80 |
| Stage 1 | 130–139 | 80–89 |
| Stage 2 | ≥ 140 | ≥ 90 |
| Crisis | > 180 | > 120 |

#### First-line pharmacotherapy
- ACE inhibitors (ramipril, lisinopril, enalapril)
- ARBs (losartan, valsartan, candesartan)
- Calcium channel blockers (amlodipine, nifedipine MR)
- Thiazide-like diuretics (indapamide, chlorthalidone)

#### Targets
- General population: < 140/90 mmHg
- High-risk (diabetes, CKD, CVD history): < 130/80 mmHg
- Elderly (> 80 years): < 150/90 mmHg

#### Combination therapy
Initiate combination if BP > 20/10 mmHg above target. Preferred combinations:
- ACE inhibitor/ARB + CCB
- ACE inhibitor/ARB + diuretic
- CCB + diuretic

**Do NOT combine**: ACE inhibitor + ARB (increased renal adverse events).

### 2. Type 2 Diabetes — Glycaemic Management

#### Targets
- HbA1c < 53 mmol/mol (7.0%) for most adults
- HbA1c < 48 mmol/mol (6.5%) if safely achievable without hypoglycaemia
- HbA1c < 64 mmol/mol (8.0%) in frail/elderly or limited life expectancy

#### Stepwise approach
1. **Metformin** — first-line for all patients (unless eGFR < 30)
2. **SGLT2 inhibitor** (empagliflozin, dapagliflozin) — add if CVD, HF, or CKD
3. **GLP-1 RA** (semaglutide, liraglutide) — add if BMI > 35 or atherosclerotic CVD
4. **DPP-4 inhibitor** (sitagliptin) — if SGLT2i/GLP-1 RA not tolerated
5. **Insulin** — if HbA1c remains above target on dual/triple therapy

#### Cardiovascular benefit
- SGLT2 inhibitors reduce HF hospitalisation by 30%
- GLP-1 RAs reduce major adverse cardiovascular events by 12–14%

### 3. Lipid Management

#### Risk-stratified LDL-C targets
| Risk Category | LDL-C Target |
|--------------|-------------|
| Very high risk | < 1.4 mmol/L AND ≥ 50% reduction |
| High risk | < 1.8 mmol/L AND ≥ 50% reduction |
| Moderate risk | < 2.6 mmol/L |
| Low risk | < 3.0 mmol/L |

#### Statin therapy
- High-intensity: atorvastatin 40–80 mg, rosuvastatin 20–40 mg
- Moderate-intensity: atorvastatin 10–20 mg, rosuvastatin 5–10 mg, simvastatin 20–40 mg

#### Combination lipid therapy
- Statin + ezetimibe if LDL-C not at target
- Add PCSK9 inhibitor (evolocumab, alirocumab) for very high risk patients not at target on maximally tolerated statin + ezetimibe

### 4. Chronic Kidney Disease

#### Staging by eGFR
| Stage | eGFR (mL/min/1.73m²) | Description |
|-------|----------------------|-------------|
| G1 | ≥ 90 | Normal or high |
| G2 | 60–89 | Mildly decreased |
| G3a | 45–59 | Mildly to moderately decreased |
| G3b | 30–44 | Moderately to severely decreased |
| G4 | 15–29 | Severely decreased |
| G5 | < 15 | Kidney failure |

#### Key interventions
- SGLT2 inhibitor for all CKD patients with eGFR 20–45 (regardless of diabetes)
- ACE inhibitor or ARB if albuminuria (ACR > 30 mg/g)
- Finerenone (non-steroidal MRA) if T2DM + CKD with residual albuminuria on RAS blockade
- BP target < 130/80 mmHg
- Avoid NSAIDs

Sources: ESC/ESH 2023, ADA Standards 2024, KDIGO 2024, ESC Dyslipidaemia 2019/2024 update
